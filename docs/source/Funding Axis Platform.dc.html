What raises an alert

A document expiring within 30 days or already lapsed. An application section left incomplete for more than 24 hours. A Bankability score that sits below the minimum grade of any funder the file is matched to. No client activity for seven days on a live file. An advisory invoice unpaid past its due date. Each alert carries an owner, and the console escalates to the admin after 48 hours.
